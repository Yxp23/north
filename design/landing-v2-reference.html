How North decides

Three answers. You set every one of them.

Approved

Fits your rules? PayPal pays.

Groceries, bills, the things you planned. North says yes in a second, and shows what is left for the week.

Wait

Your rule says wait? I save it.

Late-night buys and big ones wait for you, and I watch the price meanwhile. Gentle rules still let you tap Buy now.

Stopped

Rent money or a scam? Never.

Some walls never move: rent and bills, your weekly money, and sellers flagged as scams. Nothing is charged.

Rules in your own words

Behind the scenes

AI suggests. Code decides.

North's AI helpers find products, check deals and read your rules. None of them can pay. Only the Money Guardian can approve a payment, and it is plain code you can read and test.
